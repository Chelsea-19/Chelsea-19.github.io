I’m Jinglin (Felix) Liang, an MSc student in Biomedical Data Science (AI) at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. I explore agents and foundation models for biology and healthcare.

My projects span genomic interpretation, drug repurposing evidence, and clinical simulation. I care about how a model’s output connects to its source—and what it can tell us about the scientific question behind it.
